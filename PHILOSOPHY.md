# PIG Philosophy

PIG borrows the curiosity of Unix culture and the pleasure of recursive acronyms. It is a parody and a homage, maintained with enough care that the joke does not have to carry the whole project.

The Pig's four freedoms, as a playful statement of intent, are:

1. The freedom to run the Pig.
2. The freedom to study how the Pig works.
3. The freedom to modify the Pig.
4. The freedom to share the Pig with others.

We sometimes call source code the **Source Genome** and copyleft **Copigleft**. This terminology is not ISO certified. These names do not create legal rights; see the [license note](README.md#about-the-general-pig-license).

## Principles

1. **Maintain the project seriously.** PIG is a cultural project with clear documentation and careful contributions.
2. **Offer parody and homage.** Do not impersonate or attack the projects that inspired it.
3. **Use the pig we have.** 🐖 is the mascot; it needs no elaborate backstory.
4. **Keep licensing clear.** The General PIG License is a fictional cultural artifact, not a vetted software license.
5. **Credit other people's work.** Preserve the license and attribution of third-party assets.
6. **Avoid needless machinery.** Every dependency, service, and configuration file needs a reason.
7. **Let jokes breathe.** A good one does not need company in every paragraph.
8. **Be understandable quickly.** A newcomer should grasp “Pig Isn't GNU” and “it's a pig” in a minute.
9. **Start where we are.** The Pig Exists.
10. **Choose the simpler pig.** Prefer the smallest sound solution that does the job.
11. **PIG is lazy.** Therefore PIG avoids unnecessary work.
12. **PIG is hungry.** Therefore snacks may be considered a build dependency.
13. **PIG likes naps.** Therefore not every feature needs to ship today.
14. **PIG dislikes complexity.** If the Pig does not understand why it exists, it probably should not exist.

Lazy does not mean careless. It means declining abstractions, rewrites, meetings, and infrastructure that create more maintenance than value. The laziest correct solution is often the best one.

**Choose the simpler pig.**
