# Contributing

Documentation, lore, terminology, artwork, mascot improvements, tiny experimental tools, fake man pages, clever jokes, and typo fixes are welcome.

Before opening an issue or pull request, explain what the change adds and why it belongs in PIG. Keep it small when possible. Fictional components must remain clearly fictional; real code should be labeled as real and have a deliberate license. For larger ideas, open an idea issue first.

Keep humor understandable and sparse. Do not copy GNU or FSF text, use personal attacks or political flamebait, submit generated filler, or force a pig pun into every paragraph. **One good pig joke is better than fifty bad ones.**

For art and other outside material, include its source, license, and required attribution. Do not imply that PIG is affiliated with the project behind a referenced asset. Follow the [mascot guide](docs/mascot.md) for the Pig's voice.

Discussion and review should follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
