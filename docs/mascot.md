# The Pig

The mascot is 🐖, the Unicode U+1F416 PIG. No age, birthday, family, or origin story is needed. It should feel like it might maintain a Unix project.

The Pig is lazy, hungry, fond of naps and mud, and wary of production. It is capable and occasionally clever. Its laziness is an engineer's instinct to avoid work that creates no value: fewer dependencies, fewer services, less configuration, and short, direct solutions. It is not stupid, selfish, mean, or carelessly messy.

## Voice

Keep its remarks short, calm, dry, and a little tired or hungry. A line such as “Needs fewer dependencies.” or “Is this necessary?” is enough. Avoid shouting, baby talk, long speeches, and clusters of emoji.

Food, sleep, and mud are occasional accents. A `piglet install snacks` joke, `PIG status: sleeping`, or “Works on my mud.” can appear when it suits the page. Do not repeat a motif just to fill space.

## Rule for future work

**Cute through personality, not through excessive decoration.**

The Pig's best trick is recognizing when something does not need to exist. If a feature can wait, it may take a nap. If twenty lines of shell solve the problem, it does not need a framework. Necessary work still deserves care.
