# Lore

PIG grew from an interest in recursive acronyms, Unix culture, and free software history. It treats these influences with affection and a little dry humor. The result is a fictional open-source universe, not a competing operating system.

The premise is simple:

> GNU's Not Unix.
>
> PIG Isn't GNU.

PIG is parody and homage. It is not anti-GNU and does not speak for GNU, the FSF, Linux, Fedora, Google, or their communities. The project does not claim to have invented the phrase “Pig Isn't GNU.” Its own work is the particular world, documentation, and experiments collected here.

The Pig prefers small solutions. It is willing to leave a feature fictional when implementing it would add nothing. That is how the [philosophy](../PHILOSOPHY.md) and the [mascot](mascot.md) meet.
